# Rubyshaders – "Enchanted Sunset"

Rubyshaders is a personal shaderpack for **Minecraft Java Edition** that gives your world a warm, slightly magical look: soft golden light, gentle violet shadows and lilac-pink nights, without going over the top on color.

This pack was created with the help of **Claude**, an AI assistant by Anthropic, which wrote the shader code based on my ideas, screenshots and feedback.

## Features
- **Enchanted color grading** – warm daylight, a golden boost at sunrise and sunset, lilac-pink nights and violet-tinted shadows. Saturation is kept soft by default.
- **Real shadows** – soft-edged shadows cast by the sun (and the moon at night), with a light violet tint.
- **Waving plants** – grass, flowers, crops, tree leaves, vines and hay bales sway in the wind, and their shadows move with them. Wind gets stronger when it rains.
- **Rounded clouds** – custom clouds with smooth, round edges, light shading and subtle cloud shadows on the ground.
- **Diamond sun & moon** – your resource pack's sun and moon rotated 45°, or an optional faceted ruby sun and moon.
- **Better Grass (fast)** – grass, podzol, mycelium, nylium, dirt paths and farmland show their top texture on every side.
- **Dynamic lights** – torches, lanterns and other light sources glow around you while you hold them.
- **Optional iron shine** – a metallic glint on iron tools, armor and blocks (off by default).
- Fixes for beacon beams, glass in hand, block-breaking cracks and slime faces.

## Settings
Everything can be adjusted from the in-game shader menu (available in Spanish and English), with three profiles: **Soft**, **Enchanted** and **Epic**.

## Requirements
- Minecraft Java Edition **26.3**
- **Iris 1.11.7** with **Sodium** (OpenGL mode – Iris does not run on Vulkan)

## Installation
1. Drop the `.zip` file (don't unzip it) into your `.minecraft/shaderpacks` folder.
2. In game, go to **Options → Video Settings → Shader Packs** and select **Rubyshaders**.
